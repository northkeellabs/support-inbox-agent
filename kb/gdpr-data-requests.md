# GDPR and privacy data requests

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Your rights

If you are in a country covered by GDPR or similar laws, you can ask Ledgerline for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, or to delete it. These requests relate to personal data held about you as a Ledgerline user. Data about your own customers is held by you as the data owner, and you handle those requests yourself.

## How to make a request

Send an email to privacy@ledgerline-demo.example from the email address registered on the account. State which right you are using: access, correction, deletion or export. For your safety, the privacy team may ask you to confirm your identity before acting.

## Response time

Ledgerline answers privacy requests within 30 days of receiving them. Support agents cannot complete these requests themselves and cannot give legal advice, but can direct you to the privacy team. Complex requests can take longer, and you are told if so.

## Deleting your account

Account owners can delete the account under Settings > Account > Close account. After closing, data is deleted from active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days. Invoice records that Ledgerline must keep by law, such as subscription receipts, can be retained for the legally required period.

## Export without a request

You do not need a formal request to take a copy of your invoice data. Use Settings > Data > Export, as described in the data export article. That route is available at any time and is usually faster than a request to the privacy team.
