# Sending invoices and payment reminders

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Send an invoice by email

Select Send on any invoice. Ledgerline emails the customer a message with the PDF attached and a secure link to view and pay online. You can edit the subject and message, add up to 3 additional recipients in CC, and send yourself a copy. Emails are sent from a Ledgerline address with your business name and your reply-to address.

## Share a link instead

If email is not suitable, choose More > Copy link. The link opens a read-only invoice page with a Pay now button if online payments are enabled. Links stay valid until the invoice is voided.

## Automatic payment reminders

Go to Settings > Invoices > Reminders. You can schedule up to 4 reminders, such as 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and 7 and 14 days after. Automatic reminders are available on Growth and Scale. Starter accounts can send a manual reminder with More > Send reminder, up to once every 24 hours per invoice.

## Edit reminder wording

Each reminder has its own template with placeholders like the customer name, invoice number, amount due and due date. A preview shows the final text. Reminders stop automatically when the invoice is marked paid or voided.

## Delivery tracking

The invoice page shows when the email was delivered and when the customer first opened it. If an email bounces, the invoice is flagged Delivery failed. Check the address on the customer record and resend. Opens are an estimate, because some email programs block the tracking pixel.
