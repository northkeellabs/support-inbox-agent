# Subscription billing and receipts

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## When you are charged

Monthly plans are charged on the same day each month as the day you subscribed. If that day does not exist in a month, for example the 31st, the charge happens on the last day of that month. Annual plans are charged once a year on the anniversary. Charges are made in USD.

## Download receipts

Go to Settings > Billing > Receipts. Every charge has a receipt with a number such as RCPT-20261, the date, the plan, the period covered and any tax. Select Download PDF next to a receipt. Receipts are also emailed to the account owner at the time of each charge.

## Update billing details

Under Settings > Billing > Billing details you can change the company name, address and tax ID printed on receipts. Changes apply to future receipts only; existing receipts cannot be rewritten. If you need a corrected receipt for a past charge, contact support with the receipt number.

## Change your payment card

Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method and select Replace card. Enter the new card details. The next renewal uses the new card. Only the account owner and users with the Billing role can see or change the payment method.

## Annual billing and renewals

Annual subscriptions renew automatically. Ledgerline emails the account owner 14 days before renewal and again 3 days before. To avoid renewing, cancel at least 1 day before the renewal date. Renewal charges follow the same 14-day refund rule as every other subscription charge.
