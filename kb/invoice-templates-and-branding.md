# Invoice templates and branding

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Choose a template

Go to Settings > Invoices > Templates. Ledgerline includes three templates named Classic, Modern and Compact. Select one and use the live preview on the right to see how a sample invoice looks. The chosen template applies to all new invoices and to existing drafts.

## Upload your logo

Under Settings > Business, select Upload logo. PNG and JPG files up to 2 MB are supported, and a square or wide image of at least 400 pixels works best. SVG logos are not currently supported, so export your logo as PNG first.

## Colours and fonts

In the template editor you can set an accent colour using a hex code and pick a font from a list of six web-safe options. The accent colour is used for the invoice title, table header and the Pay now button. Custom fonts cannot be uploaded.

## Custom fields and footer

Add up to 5 custom fields, such as a purchase order number, from Settings > Invoices > Custom fields. They appear under the invoice header. The footer holds up to 300 characters and is a good place for registration numbers or bank details.

## Plan limits

Free accounts can use the Classic template only and a small Ledgerline (Demo) credit line is shown at the bottom of invoices. Starter and above can use all templates and remove the credit line. Removing the credit line takes effect on invoices sent after you upgrade.
