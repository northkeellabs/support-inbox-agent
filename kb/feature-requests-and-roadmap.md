# Feature requests and roadmap

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## How to submit a request

Go to Settings > Feedback > New request. Describe the problem you are trying to solve rather than only the feature you imagine, and say how often it affects you. You can also vote for existing requests on the same page. Each account can vote for up to 10 requests at a time.

## What happens next

The product team reads every request and reviews the most voted ones every quarter. You will get an email when the status of a request you submitted or voted for changes, for example from Open to Under consideration, Planned or Shipped. Not every request is accepted.

## No dates from support

Support agents cannot promise that a feature will be built or give a delivery date. The roadmap is a statement of direction and not a commitment, and priorities can change. If you need a feature to decide on a purchase, please make your decision based on what Ledgerline does today.

## Under consideration

Items currently marked Under consideration include a mobile app for iOS and Android, estimates and quotes that convert to invoices, and a native bank feed for automatic payment matching. Being under consideration does not mean they will ship.

## Bug or feature

A bug is something that used to work or does not behave as the help article describes. If you are unsure, contact support and the team will classify it. Reports of bugs are handled separately from feature requests, as explained in the known issues article.
