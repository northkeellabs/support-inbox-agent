# Pricing and plans

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Plans at a glance

Ledgerline (Demo) has four plans, priced in USD. Free costs $0 and includes 5 invoices per month and 1 user. Starter is $12 per month with unlimited invoices, up to 3 users, recurring invoices and multi-currency. Growth is $39 per month and adds up to 10 users, automatic reminders, the API and webhooks. Scale is $99 per month and adds unlimited users, SSO/SAML and an audit log.

## Annual billing

Choosing annual billing saves the equivalent of 2 months: you pay for 10 months and get 12. That works out to $120 per year for Starter, $390 per year for Growth and $990 per year for Scale. Annual plans are charged once, at the start of the term, and renew automatically unless cancelled.

## Feature differences

Key differences by plan:

- Free: 5 invoices per month, Classic template only, 1 user.
- Starter: unlimited invoices, all templates, recurring invoices, multi-currency.
- Growth: API access, webhooks, automatic payment reminders, extra numbering sequences.
- Scale: SSO/SAML, audit log, priority queue for support replies.

## Taxes on your subscription

Prices are shown without sales tax or VAT. Where local rules require it, tax is added at checkout and shown on your receipt. You can add a tax ID to your billing details at Settings > Billing > Billing details so it appears on receipts.

## Free plan limits

On Free, invoice number 6 in a calendar month cannot be sent until the next month or until you upgrade. Existing invoices, customers and reports stay available. Counting resets at 00:00 UTC on the first day of the month.
