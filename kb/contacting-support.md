# Contacting support

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Support hours

The support team is available Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00 UTC. Messages sent outside those hours are answered the next business day. There is no weekend or holiday coverage, and phone support is not offered.

## How to reach us

Use the Contact support form under Help > Contact support inside the app, or email support@ledgerline-demo.example from your account email. Messages sent from the form include your account details automatically, which speeds up the answer.

## What to include

To get a faster answer, include:

1. Your account email and, if relevant, the invoice number.
2. What you expected and what happened.
3. Screenshots, with card numbers and passwords hidden.
4. The browser and operating system, for display issues.

## Response times

Most messages get a first reply within 1 business day. Scale accounts are prioritised in the queue. Urgent problems affecting many invoices should say so in the subject line. Replies come from a named team member, and replies from automated assistants are marked as drafts reviewed by staff.

## What support cannot do

Support cannot promise refunds, which are decided by the billing team, and cannot disable 2FA or change the account owner, which require identity verification by the security team. Support will never ask for your password or full card number. We also cannot share information about other customers' accounts.

## Self-service first

Many questions are answered faster in the help center. Billing questions are covered in the subscription billing and receipts article, and sign-in problems in the two-factor authentication and account security articles. Searching the help center from the Help menu inside the app shows the most relevant articles as you type.
