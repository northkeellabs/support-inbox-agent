# Account security and suspicious activity

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Protect your account

Use a unique password of at least 12 characters and turn on two-factor authentication under Settings > Security. Review who has access in Settings > Users at least once a quarter and remove people who no longer need it. Ledgerline staff will never ask for your password or a 2FA code.

## Check recent sign-ins

Go to Settings > Security > Sign-in history to see the last 90 days of sign-ins with the date, approximate location and browser. Select Sign out all sessions to end every active session at once. Scale accounts also have an audit log under Settings > Security > Audit log.

## Suspicious login or email

Ledgerline emails you when a sign-in comes from a new device or country. If you did not do it, change your password immediately from the sign-in page using Forgot password, sign out all sessions, and report it to support. Emails claiming to be from Ledgerline that ask you to log in through a link should be forwarded to support instead of clicked.

## Compromised API key or account

If an API key may be exposed, revoke it under Settings > Developers > API keys right away. If you suspect an account takeover, report it through the contact form with the subject Security. These reports are handled by the security team, and support agents cannot make security changes such as changing the account owner or disabling 2FA on your behalf.

## Changing the account owner

The account owner email is changed only after identity verification by the security team. Contact support and state the current and the new owner email. Support will pass your request to the security team, who will reply with the steps and decide the outcome.
