# Accepting online payments

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Connect a payment provider

Go to Settings > Payments > Connect and follow the steps to link your payment provider account. You will be asked for business details and a bank account for payouts. Connection is available on all plans, including Free. Review of the connection usually takes one business day.

## What customers see

Once connected, each emailed invoice and shared link shows a Pay now button. Customers can pay by card and, where your provider supports it, by bank debit. The customer sees a receipt page after payment and also receives an email receipt.

## Fees and payouts

Ledgerline does not add fees on top of your provider's charges for payments. Your provider sets its own processing fees and payout schedule, usually 2 to 5 business days. Fees are shown in your provider dashboard, not inside Ledgerline.

## Automatic matching

When a customer pays online, the invoice is marked Paid within a minute and a payment record is created. Partial payments are supported: enable Allow partial payments on the invoice, and the customer can choose an amount. The invoice stays Partially paid until the balance is cleared.

## Troubleshooting

If the Pay now button is missing, check that the connection status in Settings > Payments reads Active and that the invoice currency is supported. If a payment was taken but the invoice is not marked paid after 15 minutes, see the article on payment failures and retries, or contact support with the invoice number.
