# Recording manual payments

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## When to record a payment

Use manual payments when a customer pays by bank transfer, cash, cheque or any method outside Ledgerline. Recording the payment updates the invoice status and stops automatic reminders. You can record a payment on any invoice that is not void, including invoices that were already marked paid by mistake, as long as you stay within the total amount due. Payment records are visible to every user with access to invoices.

## How to record a payment

Follow these steps:

1. Open the invoice and select Record payment.
2. Enter the amount, the date received and the method.
3. Add an optional reference such as a transfer ID.
4. Select Save.

## Partial and overpayments

If the amount is lower than the balance, the invoice becomes Partially paid and shows the remaining balance. If the amount is higher, Ledgerline stores the excess as a customer credit that you can apply to a future invoice from Customers > Credit. Credit does not expire.

## Undo or edit a payment

Open the invoice and select the payment in the Payments list, then choose Delete payment. The invoice returns to its previous status and reminders resume if enabled. Edits and deletions are recorded in the invoice history with your name and time.

## Bulk import of payments

Growth and Scale accounts can import payments from a bank statement CSV at Payments > Import. Ledgerline suggests matches using the amount and any invoice number it finds in the reference, and you confirm each match before it is saved. Imports are limited to 500 rows per file.
