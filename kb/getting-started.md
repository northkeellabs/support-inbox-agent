# Getting started with Ledgerline

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Create your account

Go to ledgerline-demo.example and choose Sign up. Enter your work email and a password of at least 12 characters, then confirm the verification email within 24 hours. Every new account starts on the Free plan, which allows 5 invoices per month and one user, and you can upgrade at any time from Settings > Billing.

## Set up your business profile

Open Settings > Business and fill in your legal business name, address, tax ID (optional) and default currency. This information is printed in the header of every invoice, so check it before you send anything. You can also upload a logo here; PNG files up to 2 MB work best.

## Add your first customer

Go to Customers > New customer. Only a name and an email address are required, but adding a billing address and a default payment term (for example Net 14 or Net 30) saves time later. You can import many customers at once from a CSV file using Customers > Import, up to 1,000 rows per file.

## Send your first invoice

The short version is this:

1. Choose Invoices > New invoice.
2. Pick the customer and add at least one line item.
3. Set the due date.
4. Select Send to email it, or Save as draft to come back later.

The full walkthrough is in the article on creating your first invoice.

## Where to find help

The help center covers every feature, and the Contact support page lists the ways to reach the team. Support is available Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00 UTC. Paid plans do not change the support hours, but replies to Scale accounts are prioritised in the queue.
