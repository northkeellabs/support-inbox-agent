# Taxes and VAT on invoices

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Create tax rates

Go to Settings > Taxes > New tax rate. Enter a name such as VAT 20%, the percentage, and whether it is inclusive or exclusive of the price. You can create up to 20 rates. Compound taxes, where one tax is calculated on top of another, can be switched on per rate.

## Apply taxes to invoices

On each line item choose a tax rate from the drop-down, or select Apply to all lines. Totals show a tax breakdown by rate, which many tax authorities require. Set a default rate in Settings > Taxes so new lines are pre-filled.

## Tax IDs and reverse charge

Store your own tax or VAT ID in Settings > Business and your customer's ID on their record. If a customer has a valid ID outside your country, you can mark the invoice Reverse charge, which sets tax to 0% and prints a note that the customer accounts for the tax. Ledgerline does not verify tax IDs against government registers.

## Tax reports

Reports > Taxes lists tax collected by rate and by period, and can be exported as CSV. The report is based on the invoice issue date by default. You can switch to cash basis, which uses the date a payment was recorded.

## Important limits

Ledgerline calculates the tax you configure. It does not decide which rate applies to you and does not file returns. For questions about your obligations, speak to a qualified tax adviser in your country.
