# Creating your first invoice

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Start a new invoice

Select Invoices > New invoice. The editor opens with today's date as the issue date and your default payment term as the due date. The invoice number is filled in automatically from your numbering sequence, for example INV-0001.

## Add customer and line items

Pick an existing customer or choose Add new customer from the drop-down. Then add line items: each needs a description, a quantity and a unit price. You can add up to 100 line items on one invoice. Discounts can be entered as a percentage or a fixed amount, either per line or on the whole invoice.

## Taxes, notes and terms

If you have configured a tax rate in Settings > Taxes, select it on each line or apply it to the whole invoice. Use the Notes field for a thank-you message or bank details, and Terms for conditions such as late payment wording. Notes and terms can be saved as defaults under Settings > Invoices.

## Preview, save or send

Select Preview to see the PDF exactly as your customer will. Choose Save as draft to keep it editable, or Send to email it immediately with a secure link to view and pay online. Once an invoice is sent you can still edit it, but the customer sees a new version and the change is recorded in the invoice history.

## Common first-time questions

Free accounts can create 5 invoices per month, and drafts count only once they are sent. You can delete a draft at any time. A sent invoice cannot be deleted, but it can be voided from the More menu, which keeps the number reserved so your sequence has no gaps.
