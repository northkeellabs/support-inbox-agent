# Refunds policy for Ledgerline subscriptions

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## What this policy covers

This policy applies to charges for your Ledgerline subscription (Starter, Growth or Scale), monthly or annual. It does not cover payments your own customers make to you through an invoice; those are between you and your customer and are refunded through your payment provider.

## The 14-day window

A subscription charge can be considered for a refund only if the request is made within 14 days of the date of that charge. After 14 days, charges are not refundable, including charges for annual plans that were barely used. Cancelling a plan stops future charges but does not by itself refund a past one.

## Who decides

Refund decisions are made by the billing team, not by support agents. Support agents cannot promise, approve or process a refund, and cannot give a timeline or guarantee of the outcome. A support agent can pass your request to the billing team along with your account email, the charge date and the amount.

## How to request a refund

To start a request:

1. Collect your account email, the invoice or receipt number from Settings > Billing > Receipts, and the date of the charge.
2. Contact support using the contact form and say clearly that you are asking for a refund.
3. The billing team reviews the request and replies by email, usually within 3 business days.

## Double charges and disputes

If you believe you were charged twice for the same period, include both receipt numbers. The billing team investigates duplicate charges and reverses confirmed duplicates. Please contact us before opening a dispute with your card issuer, because a dispute can delay resolution and may lead to the account being locked while it is reviewed.
