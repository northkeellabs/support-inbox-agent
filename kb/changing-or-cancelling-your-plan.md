# Changing or cancelling your plan

> Fictional help article for Ledgerline (Demo), a made-up invoicing app used in a demo.

## Upgrade your plan

Go to Settings > Billing > Change plan and choose the new plan. When you upgrade in the middle of a billing period, the new features are available straight away and you are charged the prorated difference for the remaining days of the period. The renewal date does not change.

## Downgrade your plan

Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep your current features until then. Before the switch, Ledgerline warns you about anything the lower plan does not support, for example API access when moving from Growth to Starter. Data is never deleted because of a downgrade, but some features become read-only.

## Switch between monthly and annual

In Settings > Billing > Change plan, select Annual or Monthly. Moving from monthly to annual starts the annual term immediately and the unused part of the current month is credited. Moving from annual to monthly takes effect at the end of the annual term.

## Cancel your subscription

Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Cancellation stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, then the account moves to the Free plan. Only the account owner and users with the Billing role can cancel.

## Cancellation and refunds

Cancelling does not automatically refund a past charge. Refunds are only considered within 14 days of a charge and are decided by the billing team, as explained in the refunds policy article. Support agents cannot promise a refund.
